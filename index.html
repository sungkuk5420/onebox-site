Why it matters

The pair ships from one warehouse,
but the listings live on several marketplaces

Double-selling → penalty

It sells on one marketplace, the others stay live

List the same pair on KREAM personal and KREAM partner at once, and the second order arrives with nothing to ship. That means a cancellation penalty and a hit to account trust.

Spreadsheet reconciliation

Every marketplace is checked separately, by hand

When receiving, shipping and adjustments are not recorded, you can never answer "why was this number what it was", and reconciliation falls apart at every settlement.

Tied-up cash

Unsold stock quietly piles up

Aging inventory, and pairs sent to KREAM's consignment warehouse with no bid, just accrue storage fees. Nobody tells you unless something is watching.

Features

Only what resale inventory needs, and all of it

We left out bundles, multi-currency and team permissions that one-person and small resale operations never use, and built what resale actually needs.

SKU ledger

Model code and size are the unit of stock. Sizes come from the catalog, so a size that does not exist never ends up in the ledger.

  • Register by product search — just size and quantity
  • Register pairs for POIZON or SOLDOUT, or not yet listed anywhere
  • Quantities are folded from the ledger — numbers never drift

Receive · bulk receive · stock count

Receive one pair, paste a purchase list from Excel to receive in bulk, or count the shelf and adjust only the difference.

  • Excel only receives — it never silently overwrites stock
  • Uncounted means unknown, not zero
  • Barcode scan receiving

AI double-sell alert AI

Compares live listings against available stock per channel. Counting per channel, not in total, means normal cross-listing is never a false alarm.

  • KREAM consignment stock excluded from cross-channel math
  • A failed lookup is shown as pending, not as zero
  • In-app alert the moment it happens

Sale detection · delisting AI

Sales on KREAM personal and partner accounts are detected about once a minute and posted to the ledger. Delisting is suggest-first: only what you approve gets pulled.

  • Modes: off / suggest (default) / auto
  • Auto runs only when every safety gate passes
  • Everything pulled is logged

Cost · margin · analytics

Suggests unit cost from your actual purchase history, and lets you query the ledger by period and type and export to Excel.

  • Missing cost stays blank, never zero
  • Lock settled periods
  • Shareable inventory link

NANBA ONE integration

Boxes that passed inspection in receiving, and pairs that came back through returns, move into the ledger with one click.

  • You pick the rows — nothing is posted automatically
  • Posted state is derived from the record, so nothing is counted twice
  • Desktop app and mobile web share one account

ONEBOX AI · built on Claude

AI that reads, explains and proposes

ONEBOX's AI features are built on Anthropic's Claude models. The rule engine counts what is over-exposed; Claude explains why and what to do in plain language. A person always approves execution.

Beta

Purchase lists · invoice photos → structured SKUs

Resellers keep purchase records as spreadsheets, chat messages and photos of invoices. Claude reads them and returns model code, size, quantity and unit cost. You check the table and press receive.

Beta

Alert explanation and next action

Instead of "CW2288-111 / 270 / over by 1", Claude tells you which channel sold when and which listing is safest to pull, with the evidence. Suggest only; execute after approval.

Planned

Natural-language inventory questions

"How many Dunk 270s received last month are still unsold?" Claude turns the question into a ledger query. Quantities always come from the ledger fold, never from the model.

Planned

Product-name normalization · model matching

The same shoe is named differently on every marketplace ("Air Force 1 Low White 07" vs "AF1 '07"). Claude maps variants to one model code so the ledger never shows one pair as two rows.

Why Claude

An inventory ledger is money-bearing data. We need long context to read full purchase histories, multimodal input for tables and invoice photos, and a model that says "I don't know" instead of guessing a quantity. Counting stays in the ledger; the model never writes to it.

What the AI does not do

  • Estimate quantities or edit the ledger. The ledger is the only producer of stock numbers.
  • Execute irreversible writes (cancelling a listing, shipping an order) on its own.
  • See marketplace tokens or passwords. Secrets never leave the user's PC.

How it works

Register, list as usual, and let AI watch the rest

Register your stock

Search a product and enter size and quantity, or paste a purchase list from Excel. Boxes that passed inspection in receiving move over with one click.

List on every marketplace as usual

ONEBOX reads your KREAM personal and partner listings automatically and layers them over the stock. POIZON and SOLDOUT pairs live in the same ledger.

AI detects and alerts

When a sale is detected it is posted to the ledger. If the same pair is still live on another channel, you get an alert immediately. Approve and it is pulled; in auto mode it is pulled once every gate passes.

Safety

This pulls real listings,
so pulling the wrong one is what we feared most

If the AI is not sure, it does not act. Anything ambiguous goes to a person.

Suggest by default, two gates for auto

The default shows what would be pulled and pulls only what you approve. Auto mode runs only when every safety condition passes.

Log first, then act

What is being pulled and why is written down before anything is sent. If the log cannot be written, nothing is cancelled.

Mass-cancel guard

If many items are flagged at once, or cancellations cluster in a short window, it stops, does nothing, and notifies you. Repeated failures suspend auto mode until a person clears it.

Unknown stays unknown, never zero

If a lookup fails, the previous result stays on screen with an error. The table is never emptied to look like zero.

FAQ

Questions

Do I need to be using NANBA ONE already?

The ledger (register, receive, adjust, query) works on the web without the desktop app. KREAM sale detection and delisting need the desktop app running. Both use the same account.

Will the AI pull my listings on its own?

The default is suggest mode: it shows what it would pull and pulls only what you approve. Even in auto mode it acts only on strongly evidenced sales, only when every safety condition passes, and it stops if several items are flagged at once.

Does it handle POIZON and SOLDOUT stock?

Registering, receiving and querying stock works regardless of marketplace. Sale detection and delisting currently support KREAM personal and partner accounts; other marketplaces will follow.

How are pairs sent to KREAM consignment counted?

They are tracked separately as "channel storage" and never mixed with own-warehouse stock. A pair in KREAM's warehouse cannot be shipped to another marketplace, so it is excluded from double-sell math.

Does the AI see my inventory data and marketplace accounts?

The AI reads only ledger quantities and records and the purchase files you upload. Marketplace tokens, cookies and passwords stay inside your PC and are never sent to our servers or to the AI. See the privacy policy (Korean).

What if it sells on two marketplaces at almost the same time?

A sale on both sides within the detection interval (about a minute) cannot be prevented. Instead it is surfaced immediately so you can handle it right away.
